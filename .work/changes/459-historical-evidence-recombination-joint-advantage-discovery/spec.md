## Outcome

Find a materially positive, sufficiently frequent, development-only natural-gas trading candidate by recombining surviving information from the entire repository history and jointly searching signal, side, regime, lifecycle, risk, sizing, execution and adaptation interactions.

## Design

1. Build an exhaustive advantage map from repository research artifacts and governed change records before any #459 scoring.
2. Classify historical evidence without rewriting it: `positive_clue`, `empirical_negative`, `null_or_inconclusive`, `underpowered`, `source_or_data_hold`, `role_unidentifiable`, `untested_mechanism`, and `governance_only`.
3. Preserve exact source file, object path, disposition/status, reason, identifiers and numeric economic metrics for every indexed record.
4. Add a curated priority layer for historically material clues that are directly actionable in the joint search; curated entries must point back to raw indexed evidence.
5. Build the joint optimizer only after the map is deterministic and tested.

- Development through 2022 only; protected 2023+ confirmation, prospective paper, SIM and LIVE stay sealed.
- Search long and short policy roles separately before recombination.
- Search interactions spanning signal/regime × lifecycle × risk/sizing × execution/adaptation; do not run isolated one-variable sweeps as the governing strategy.

- Preserve simple nested controls and exact trial/configuration accounting.
